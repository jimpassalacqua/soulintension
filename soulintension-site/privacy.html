Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Soulintension is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name and email address when you submit the contact form
  • Your email address if you subscribe to our newsletter
  • Any message content you choose to share through the contact form

If you accept cookies, we also collect anonymized analytics data through Google Analytics 4, including pages visited, time on site, and general geographic region. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your messages and inquiries
  • To send newsletters if you have subscribed and consented
  • To understand how visitors use the site and improve the experience

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

We use cookies only with your consent. If you accept cookies, Google Analytics 4 places anonymized tracking cookies to help us understand site usage. If you decline, no tracking cookies are set and no analytics data is collected. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the site.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Netlify: website hosting and form processing
  • Google Analytics 4: anonymized site analytics (only with your consent)
  • Kit (formerly ConvertKit): newsletter management

Data Retention

Form submissions are retained in Netlify's dashboard and forwarded to our email. Newsletter subscribers are retained in Kit until you unsubscribe. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us through the connect page.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out through the connect page.
