what I offer

Here when you're ready

Whether you're an individual looking for a safe conversation, a church leader wanting to educate your congregation, or a community ready to go deeper, I'd love to talk about how I can help.

01 / one-on-one

A safe space to be heard

Sometimes you just need someone who gets it. I offer personal conversations for LGBT individuals, parents of LGBT kids, and others personally touched by the lives of LGBT people close to them who want a safe, honest, empathetic space to share what they're carrying and be heard without judgment or agenda.

This isn't therapy and I'm not a counselor. It's a conversation with someone who's been there and wants to walk alongside you for a bit.

let's talk about this
02 / educational event

Equip your congregation to show up well

I facilitate educational events for churches, small groups, and ministry teams who want to be better prepared for honest and loving conversations with LGBT people in their lives and communities.

Built around the Grace and Truth curriculum from the Center for Faith, Sexuality and Gender, these sessions are grounded, practical, and designed to move people from uncertainty to confidence.

let's talk about this
03 / weekend conference

Go deeper with your community

A Friday evening and full Saturday conference for churches that want to move beyond surface-level awareness and into genuine, compassionate engagement with LGBT followers of Jesus.

Designed for leadership teams, small groups, and congregation members who are ready to wrestle honestly with the questions and come out better equipped to love well.

04 / something else

Don't see what you need?

Every person and every community is different. If none of the above quite fits what you're looking for, let's have a conversation about it. I'm open to exploring what would actually be useful for your specific situation.
