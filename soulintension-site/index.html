LGBT and Jesus / Sonoma County and Beyond

Somewhere between polarization and surrender

Is there a space, a mindset, a posture, where we can meet as human souls to be heard, loved, and respected?

Grace, truth, empathy, and hope, for LGBT followers of Jesus.

find your people my story

"My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness." 2 Corinthians 12:9

what is this

a place for the honest questions

Soulintension exists for LGBT people who are still reaching toward Jesus, even when the path is not clear and the church has not always made it easy. A place where faith and honest questions can sit in the same room together.

who it is for

Gay, lesbian, bisexual, or questioning

Whether you're spiritually curious, spiritually hungry, or spiritually exhausted. Not ready to give up on Jesus, even if the church has sometimes made that feel impossible.

what we do

Honest conversation and real presence

Grace, truth, empathy, and hope. A community trying to walk faithfully with God in the tension. Not pretending the hard questions are not real, and not facing them alone.

what it's not

A place that asks you to disappear

This isn't a space that will tell you your sexuality makes you less loved by God. Come as you are. Bring your questions, your doubts, and an open heart.

community gathering

a mid-week place to belong

For LGBT individuals, straight allies, and anyone who's found themselves on the edges of church life. We meet mid-week so you can attend both gatherings.

when Mid-week evening. Details shared when you reach out.
where Sonoma County, CA. Location shared on request.
what to expect Honest conversation, shared Scripture, no performance required.
